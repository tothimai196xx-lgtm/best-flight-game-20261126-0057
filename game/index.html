How to fly

W/S throttle   ←→ bank & turn   ↑↓ pitch   Shift boost   G landing gear   F flaps   C cockpit / chase camera   M mute   P pause   mouse: drag to steer, wheel: throttle. Touch buttons work on mobile.

Landing: gear down, flaps down, under 90 kt, descend gently onto the runway centreline. A hard touchdown or a water landing ends the run. Rings pay 250 pts each, a perfect landing up to 1,000 pts plus a time bonus.
